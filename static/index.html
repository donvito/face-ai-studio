Liveness (anti-spoofing)

InsightFace 2.0's optional RGB liveness addon scores each face as live or presentation attack (printed photo, screen replay). In normal mode, faces that fail are kept but skipped for recognition.

Pick a photo

Album clustering

No labels needed: every face across the sample album is embedded, then grouped by similarity. This is how photo apps build "People" albums.

Video privacy (PrivateFrame)

InsightFace 2.0's PrivateFrame detects and tracks faces in a video, then blurs or mosaics them. It can blur everyone, only selected people, or everyone except selected people.

Person tracking (PersonAnalysis)

InsightFace 2.1's PersonAnalysis finds bodies and faces in each frame and matches them to registered people by face, then by body appearance (ReID) when the face is not visible.
